# 0.8.1

Tuya Smart and LSC Smart Connect email/password sign-in now works without an
Android app package.

- Include the matching client configuration, checked with the vendor before
  submitting an encrypted password. Optional overrides remain under Advanced options.
- Reduce repeated device-control style rebuilds during rapid brightness and power changes.
- Add local sign-in diagnostics that omit account credentials and verification codes.
- Consolidate outdated research and audit documents into current setup and integration guides.

Fresh Tuya, LSC and Govee sign-ins were verified with real accounts, including
Govee email verification.
